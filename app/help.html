For volunteers

Joining an organization

You sign up for an organization's shifts once you're a member of it. There's no password: you sign in with a 6-digit code we email you, or with Continue with Google.

  • With an invite link: open it. The page says Join and the organization's name. Sign in, fill in your profile and Save profile, and you're in.
  • With a code: tap Sign up on the home page (or Join in the menu), then Have an invitation code?. Enter the code and tap Join.
  • Invited by email: sign in with the email address the invitation went to.
  • No invitation? On Join Sevak, search Find your organization by name or city, pick it, add a note if you like, and tap Ask to join. You can sign up once they approve you; Sevak tells you when they do.

Already signed in? Use Join an organization on My profile.

Signing up for shifts

  1. Open Opportunities. My organizations shows events of the organizations you belong to.
  2. Tap an event, then tick one or more shifts. For events over several days, pick a day at the top.
  3. Tap Continue, check your shifts, then Confirm sign-up.

You'll get a confirmation email. If two shifts you picked overlap, Sevak warns you.

Changing or cancelling

Open My sign-ups, then Manage or cancel on the event. Tap Cancel next to a shift to give up your spot, or Sign up for more shifts to add one. The organizers see the spot is open again.

Adding to your calendar

After you sign up (and on Manage or cancel), Add to your calendar offers two ways: Download .ics file works with Apple Calendar, Outlook and most other calendar apps; Google Calendar adds one shift at a time. Times are in the event's time zone.

Logging your hours

  • On the day: from an hour before your shift, open My sign-ups and tap Check in. Tap Check out when you're done; Sevak works out your hours.
  • Afterwards: tap Log hours and enter the times (From and to) or How long, then Save hours.

Your hours say Waiting for approval until the event's organizers approve them. If they're Not approved, you'll see why, and you can edit them to send them again.

Asking for a certificate

  1. On My sign-ups, go to Certificates and tap Request a certificate.
  2. Choose the organization and the period (or All my hours, This year, Last year, Last 12 months), then Send request.

It covers your approved hours only. When an Admin issues it, you get an email, and it's saved in your Documents.

Your privacy

  • Other volunteers and the public never see your name or details, only how many spots are left.
  • The Admins of an organization you belong to see your details. The people running an event, and a task's lead, see the name, phone and email you gave when you signed up for it.
  • Organizations never see your street address. Your Documents are seen only by you.

For organizations

Getting started

  1. Sign in and fill in your profile. New to Sevak? On Join Sevak, choose Setting up a new organization? Start here.
  2. On My profile, tap Set up your organization. Fill in its tabs (About, Address, Status, Contact) and Save profile. You're its Admin.
  3. Invite your team: on the Organization page's Team tab, tap + Add a team member, enter their name and email, choose Admin or Coordinator, and Save profile. They get access when they sign in with that email.
  4. Invite volunteers: on Volunteers, under Invite volunteers, Copy the invite link (it has a short code too) and share it, or Invite by email and Send invitations. People who ask to join appear under Requests to join with Approve and Decline, unless you tick Approve requests automatically.

Creating an event

On Events, tap + Event. The editor has four steps; Next moves on, and you can Save draft at any step.

  1. Details: the name, a description, and its main event if it has one.
  2. When & where: the dates (up to 31 days), the time zone, and the place: In person, Online or Shared later.
  3. Tasks: each job volunteers will do, with its shifts (day, start, end, how many volunteers). Or start from a common task fills one in for you.
  4. Review: check everything, choose who can see it and who approves hours (the event admin), then Publish.
  • Main events group related events, such as a festival and its rehearsals. Tap + Main event, save it, then add each event with + Sub-event. A one-off event doesn't need one.
  • Location shared later is for private venues, or when you email the address to people who sign up. No address is shown; volunteers see your note, or "Contact your event admin for location details."

Before Sevak verifies your organization

Only verified organizations list public events, so people can't find a new organization's events by browsing. Until Sevak verifies you, every event stays private, even one set to Public. To ask, an Admin taps Request verification at the top of the Organization page, then Send request. Sevak emails you when it's decided.

Meanwhile, share your events yourself:

  • In the event editor, Open the volunteer sign-up page and share its link by WhatsApp, text or email.
  • Members who joined through your invite link or code can see the event and sign up.
  • People who haven't joined see a message asking them to join first, so send your invite link along with the event.

Task leads

Each task can have a Task lead who runs it on the day. An Admin chooses one in the Tasks step, from your team and your active volunteers. The lead sees who signed up for that task's shifts, with their phone and email, under Tasks you lead on My sign-ups, and nothing else.

Approving hours

On Volunteers, Hours to approve lists the hours volunteers logged. For each: Approve, Adjust… (approve a different amount) or Don't approve… with a reason the volunteer sees. Approve all does a batch. The event admin approves an event's hours, and Admins can approve any. Nobody approves their own hours: an Admin's need a different Admin.

Issuing certificates

Volunteers ask for certificates; they're never issued automatically. On Volunteers, under Certificate requests, an Admin can Preview a certificate, then Issue it or Decline… with a reason. It's signed with your name, title and signature: set them once on My profile under Certificates you sign. Issuing emails the volunteer and saves the certificate in their Documents and each event's Documents.

Importing from Zeffy

  1. On Events, tap From Zeffy.
  2. An Admin pastes your organization's Zeffy API key (in Zeffy: Settings → Integrations → API) and taps Connect Zeffy.
  3. Pick a Zeffy event and tap Start from this. Sevak starts a draft with its title, description, dates and link. Add the place, check the time zone, and add tasks and shifts.

If the event changes on Zeffy, use Refresh from Zeffy in the editor. Sevak only reads your events from Zeffy, never donors or payments, and an Admin can Disconnect at any time.

On the day of your event

A checklist for hosts, all from your phone:

  1. Open the event's roster: on Events, tap Roster on the event. It lists everyone on each shift, with their phone and email.
  2. Tap Check in as each person arrives (Undo if you tapped the wrong one).
  3. For someone who turns up without signing up, tap + Add someone on their shift. Their name is enough; with their email they also get a confirmation.
  4. Tap Check out when they leave: Sevak records their hours from the check-in, and they're approved. Your own hours wait for another Admin.
  5. Anyone you didn't check out can Log hours on My sign-ups afterwards; approve those on Volunteers under Hours to approve.

Roles: who can do what

One person can have several roles: for example a volunteer with one organization and an Admin of another.

Sevak roles and what each can do
RoleWhat they can do
VolunteerJoins organizations, signs up for their shifts, records hours and asks for certificates. Sees only their own details, history and certificates.
CoordinatorOn an organization's team. Runs its events and dashboard and adds documents. Can open the organization's profile but not change it or its team, and doesn't see its volunteer list. As an event's event admin, sees its roster and approves its hours.
AdminEverything for the organization: its profile, team and verification, events, volunteers and requests to join. Approves hours for any of its events and issues its certificates. Only Admins delete events.
Task leadA duty, not a role: leads one task on the day. Sees who signed up for that task's shifts and how to reach them, and nothing else.
Event adminA duty, not a role: chosen for each event from the people who run the organization (any Admin if nobody is chosen). Approves that event's hours, and sees its roster.
