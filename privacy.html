Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 13, 2026

Sevak, a service of The ANB Group ("we", "us"), helps organizations run volunteer events: they publish events with tasks and shifts, volunteers sign up and record their hours, and hosts approve them. Sevak is in a live beta, used by real organizations and volunteers. This policy explains what information we keep, who sees it, and your choices. Two commitments come first:

  • We never sell your data. We don't sell, rent or trade personal information to anyone, and we don't use it for advertising.
  • Data belongs to the host organization. Information collected through an organization's events, such as sign-ups, rosters, attendance, hours and certificates, belongs to that organization. Sevak stores and processes it only to provide the service to them.

1. What we collect

  • Your profile: your name, email address and mobile phone (and whether it's on WhatsApp), and anything optional you add: a photo, gender, an address, affiliations (such as a school or Scout troop), your usual availability, the services you can offer, and your "Get to know me" answers (about me, your passion, your certifications, how you can help).
  • Volunteering: the organizations you join or ask to join (with any note you send), the shifts you sign up for and cancel, your check-in and check-out times, the hours you log and whether they're approved, and the certificates you ask for and receive.
  • Documents: files you upload to My profile, an organization or an event: PDFs, pictures, Word, Excel and PowerPoint files, and text files, up to 10 MB each. Certificates issued to you are saved there too.
  • Organizations: the organization's name, type, website, address, description, WhatsApp link, charity or registration details, primary contact, and its team (each person's name, email, optional phone and role). To be verified, an organization sends us its registration number, website, contact email and an optional note, and can add documents such as registration papers. Admins who issue certificates add a title and can draw a signature.
  • Signing in: we email you a one-time code or link. If you choose Continue with Google, Sevak receives only your email address from Google, not your contacts, files or anything else in your Google account, and keeps no Google passwords or tokens.
  • Technical information: to keep Sevak secure and stop abuse, we keep short-lived records such as IP addresses and how often something was tried (for example sign-in attempts), and the server keeps basic logs.

2. Cookies and tracking

Sevak uses one cookie, only to keep you signed in. Your session ends after 24 hours without use, and after 30 days at most. Sevak also keeps a copy of your own data in your browser's storage so pages load quickly; it's cleared when you sign out. There is no advertising, no analytics and no tracking from other companies.

3. How we use it

We use information only to run Sevak: to manage sign-ups and hours, send confirmations, updates, invitations and certificates, keep the service secure, and fix problems. The host organization decides how it uses its volunteers' information for its own events.

4. Who can see what

  • You see your own details, sign-ups, hours, documents and certificates. Volunteers never see other volunteers' details.
  • An organization's Admins see the volunteers of their organization: the name, email, photo, services and "About me" of people who join or ask to join, and the name, email and phone given on each sign-up for its events. Other optional profile details may be shared with organizations whose events you sign up for; your profile says so next to each one. Organizations never see your street address.
  • Coordinators (team members who help run events) see the roster only of the events they run as event admin.
  • Task leads see the roster of the task they lead: who signed up for its shifts, with the name, phone and email they gave.
  • The public sees only counts, such as "4 of 6 spots left", never who signed up. Private events are never listed. Anyone with a certificate's number can check it's genuine and see the name, organization, period, hours and events on it.
  • Your documents are seen only by you. An organization's and its events' documents are seen only by that organization's team.
  • Sevak staff can see information when they need to: to review verification requests, help with a problem you report, or stop abuse.

5. Service providers

We use a few companies to run Sevak. They may use data only to provide their service to us.

  • Google Cloud hosts Sevak, its database and uploaded files, in the United States.
  • Resend delivers Sevak's emails.
  • Google signs you in, only if you choose Continue with Google.
  • Zeffy, only if an organization's Admin connects it: Sevak reads the organization's ticketed events from Zeffy (title, description, dates and link) to start events. It doesn't read donors, payments or contacts.

6. How long we keep it

We keep information while your account, or the organization it belongs to, exists. When an organization is deleted, its events, its volunteer list and its Zeffy connection are removed; sign-ups with their hours, certificates and personal documents stay with the people they belong to. The database is backed up every night, and backups are kept for 7 days and then expire. Deleted information can stay in a backup until it expires.

7. Your choices

  • You can see and change your profile, sign-ups, documents and certificates at any time.
  • You can leave an organization from My profile, and delete your profile and your documents with Delete my profile.
  • To have your account deleted completely, or to ask for a copy of your data, email privacy@theanbgroup.com. We remove your login, your profile and your documents. Records that belong to an organization, such as attendance and certificates it issued, stay with that organization.
  • To correct or delete what an organization holds about you, contact that organization. We'll help them act on your request.

8. Children

Sevak is for people 13 and over. Volunteers under 18 should have a parent's or guardian's permission to volunteer. Each organization is responsible for its own age rules for its events. We don't knowingly allow accounts for children under 13; if we learn of one, we delete it.

9. Security

Data is encrypted in transit and at rest, and the database accepts only encrypted connections. Uploaded files are kept private and served only to people allowed to see them. Sign-in codes and sessions are stored only as one-way hashes. Access is limited to the people who need it.

10. Changes to this policy

We'll update this policy when Sevak or our practices change. The date at the top shows when it last changed.

11. Contact

Questions about privacy: privacy@theanbgroup.com. Anything else: support@theanbgroup.com.
